By the end of this week you will be able to:

- describe single-qubit unitary transformations and how they affect the state of a single qubit
- represent multi-qubit states and how to represent the effect of unitary transformations on them
- explain the effect of measurements on multi-qubit states, in a way that depends on the measurement outcome
- describe the difference between states and product states, in mathematical terms
