### Interactive Optional Resource
#### Bloch sphere simulator
- https://bloch.kherb.io/

**Purpose**

This interactive activity is designed to help you visualise the concept of the Bloch Sphere and how it can help you to understand the action of gates. It’s optional but highly recommended for deepening your understanding.

**What to do**
- Follow the link, which will take you to the simulation. 
- Try adjusting the parameters and investigating different scenarios. 
- Reflect on how this links to the theory you’ve just learned. 

**Tips for success**
- Select the ‘Explanation’ button, which will take you to the ‘Mathematical background’ and ‘How to use this tool’ sections. 
- The ‘INIT’ button will let you investigate again with a certain start state. 
- Press on the sphere and rotate to see from different angles. 
- If you’re stuck, revisit the related content.

**Reflect and connect**

After completing the activity, consider the following: 
- Can you predict how a gate is represented on the Bloch sphere, given its matrix representation? 
- How does this connect to the theory? 
- Can you think of a real-world application of this concept?
