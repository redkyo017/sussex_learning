This week, we explore the possibilities of quantum information, which differ from what can be done with classical information.

As with previous weeks, there is a substantial amount of study material. Please keep this in mind while planning your studying. If you have any questions, feel free to take advantage of the Discussion board for assistance and engaging discussions.

By the end of this week you will be able to:

- use QAAL to describe quantum procedures and operations
- outline the principles of quantum teleportation and identify the role of entanglement and classical communication
- explain phase kicks and phase estimation in quantum computing.
