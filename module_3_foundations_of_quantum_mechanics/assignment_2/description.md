### Essay
Write a short essay (1,200 words) on the material from Week 6 on the topic of the **Stern–Gerlach experiment**.

The essay should cover the following topics:
* Applications of the Stern–Gerlach experiment in quantum technologies, including the use of spin states for quantum computing, quantum sensing and quantum communication.
* Challenges and opportunities in utilising the Stern–Gerlach experiment for practical applications in quantum technologies, such as scalability, noise and control issues, and also the potential for future advancements in the field.

The essay should include an introduction and a conclusion and:
* Examples and case studies of real-world applications of the Stern–Gerlach experiment in quantum technologies.
* A critical analysis of the strengths and limitations of the experiment for different uses.
* A range of sources, including textbooks, research papers, review articles and online resources, to provide a comprehensive and balanced overview of the topic.

Please use the numerical referencing system and ensure that all pages are numbered sequentially.
