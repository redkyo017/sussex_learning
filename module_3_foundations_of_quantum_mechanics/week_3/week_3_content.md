## Intro
Physics is based on experimental observations and quantitative measurements. In quantum mechanics, the act of measurement affects the system that we are measuring and fundamentally changes it. In Week 3 we will present three postulates that deal with measurement. This will allow us to highlight fundamental differences between classical and quantum physics.

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

explain the importance of measurement in quantum mechanics
explain the probabilistic nature of quantum mechanics using mathematical language
outline some historical interpretations of quantum mechanics.

#### Problem 2

- the nucleus
- a distance $a_0$ from the nucleus.

As the region of interest is so small, even on the scale of the atom, we can ignore the variation of $\psi$ within it.
