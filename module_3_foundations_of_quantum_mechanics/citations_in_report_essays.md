With the incoming submission of Essay, I thought I might highlight two web pages that can help you with citations.

The first one, https://search.crossref.org/,Links to an external site. allows you to find the so-called DOI (Digital Object Identifier), which is like a unique ID for all books, articles and other written material that has an online presence.

The second web page is "DOI Citation Formatter", (https://citation.crosscite.org/Links to an external site.), where you can get an appropriately formatted citation by supplying the DOI of the book or article.

For example, if you want to quote the book "Quantum Mechanics for Electrical Engineers" by Dennis M. Sullivan, you can first find its DOI on crossref (10.1002/9781118169780), and then put that DOI in "DOI Citation Formatter" to obtain a nicely formatted citation:

[1] D. M. Sullivan, Quantum Mechanics for Electrical Engineers (John Wiley & Sons, Inc., 2012). 

In "DOI Citation Formatter", I recommend using the formatting style "american-physics-society".

Note that sometime the DOI will appear as: "doi.org/10.1002/9781118169780". In that case, remove "doi.org/" string and provide just the characters that follow it to the "DOI Citation Formatter".
