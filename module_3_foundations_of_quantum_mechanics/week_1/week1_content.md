## Intro
The aim of Week 1 is to give you the historical background into which quantum mechanics was born and to introduce you briefly to the conventions and mathematical tools that we will use in this module. By the end of this week, you will have acquired a broader picture of what we will be doing during the whole module. As we progress in the following weeks, you will deepen your knowledge of topics that we introduce in Week 1 and apply that knowledge in the context of quantum technologies.

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

explain the concepts of wave functions and operators
perform basic mathematical operations with vectors
describe the role of eigenfunctions and eigenvalues.
