I am sharing a LaTeX template for the Assessed Problem Set, which is due in Week 4, together with a short LaTeX tutorial that I prepared (see direct links below).

You are not required to use LaTeX, but I would encourage you to try it, particularly if you expect to continue working with mathematical or technical material.
