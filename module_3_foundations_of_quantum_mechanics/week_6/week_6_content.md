# Week 6: Stern-Gerlach experiment
## Intro
By studying various aspects and concepts of quantum mechanics over the past weeks, we have given you the ammunition required to explore a world through a new lens. In Week 6, we really begin proper quantum mechanics, in the sense that we are going to describe a quantum mechanical phenomenon in a completely quantum mechanical way. For that purpose, we will consider the famous experiment of Otto Stern and Walther Gerlach from early 1922 [1]Links to an external site.. The Stern-Gerlach experiment demonstrated that the spatial orientation of angular momentum is quantised – similarly to what we have seen for energy levels of the quantum harmonic oscillator.
By the end of this week, you will be able to:
give a high-level explanation of the quantisation of the angular momentum in the context of Stern-Gerlach experiment
analyse and predict outcomes of a repeated measurement.
