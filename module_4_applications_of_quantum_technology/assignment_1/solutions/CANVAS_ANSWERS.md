**Q9 (10 pts)**
Therefore the Moon's tidal effect changes g by about 2GM R_E / r³ ≈ 1.1×10^-6 m/s² (against g when the Moon is overhead), about 1000 times the interferometer's resolution. The Moon's direct pull GM/r² = 3.3×10^-5 m/s² is not observed, because the Earth falls with it. Full workings: uploaded PDF (Question 12), section "Question 9".

**Q10 (10 pts)**
