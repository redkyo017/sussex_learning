# Week 3: Quantum sensors part 2: Inertial forces: Introduction
## Intro
This week, we will cover quantum sensors based on atom interferometry and their applications. In recent decades, atom interferometry has developed rapidly and matured to a versatile tool to measure inertial forces with ultrahigh precision. It is used for fundamental research in particle physics, general relativity and cosmology. At the same time, it has also been applied in areas of metrology, geophysics, civil engineering, oil and minerals exploration, navigation and space.

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

explain the concept of atom interferometry and how an atomic gravimeter works
compare the performance of classical and quantum gravimeters
explain the analogy between optical and atom interferometry
describe the response factors of optical and atom interferometry
explain how different types of atom interferometers work and provide examples of their application areas.
