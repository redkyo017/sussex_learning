# Week 4: Quantum communication: Introduction
## Intro
Encrypting messages and transmitting them secretly between remote partners has been an important task for millennia. Quantum physics provides, for the first time, a method for secret communication that is intrinsically secure by the laws of nature. This possibility has sparked a great interest in developing and selling quantum communication tools. During this week, you will learn about the concepts and key implementation protocols and the status of practical implementations and the barriers on the way towards truly secure and fast communication.
By the end of this week, you will be able to:
explain the basics of quantum communication encoding, protocols and hardware
describe what current technology enables quantum communication and the limitations of the quantum channels and bandwidth
describe the sectors that are investing in quantum technology and the aspects of quantum communication that are of interest
create a timeline of what has been achieved in quantum communication and future developments.
