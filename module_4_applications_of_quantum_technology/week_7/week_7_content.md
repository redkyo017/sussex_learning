# Week 7: Comparing different quantum technologies: Introduction
## Intro
While the discovery of quantum physics shook the world of scholars more than one hundred years ago, it has been the continuous effort in the laboratories over many decades that has brought the set of available tools to the point where quantum technologies have become viable. After having studied different versions of those technologies and some of their main applications, a comparative look at the state of the practical developments and beginning market penetration is crucial. We will discuss aspects of this in the concluding week of this module.

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

differentiate between the states of development of different quantum technologies
understand what the main challenges are in implementing quantum technologies
describe key flagship success stories of quantum technologies working in the application space.
