# Week 2: Quantum sensors part 1: Clocks and magnetometers: Introduction
## Intro
While quantum technologies hold the promise of widespread improvements in many application areas, quantum magnetometers and clocks are arguably the most advanced in terms of their physical implementation, validation and commercialisation. During this week, we will study the operating principles of these devices and gain insights into some key applications. There are numerous exciting prospects for a range of sectors from navigation to healthcare, where the new disruptive technologies have already begun to enter the market space.

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

explain the importance of precision timing, magnetic field sensing and their wide-ranging applications
describe the current state of the art conventional sensors
outline cases which precision timers and ultra-sensitive magnetic field sensors enable.
