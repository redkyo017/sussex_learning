# Week 6: Quantum computing: Introduction
## Intro
Quantum computing has attracted a lot of attention over recent years. While the notion that quantum computers will solve all problems of modern-day computing is false, there are specific classes of problems where classically impossible quantum algorithms can provide radically new solutions with relevance in many application areas. The vision of a quantum computer has sparked fascination and investment alike. As part of this module, we will highlight key aspects of operating principles, the state of implementation and possible applications.

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

describe the basic operating principle of quantum computers
understand the key approaches to implementations of quantum computing
explain the benefits of quantum computers to applications.
