# Week 1: Types of quantum technologies and applications: Introduction
## Intro
This week, will cover an overview of key quantum technology types. We will discuss quantum sensing, communication, simulation and computing. This will include the basic operation principles in general terms, as well as key specifics for each of the four areas. We will also touch upon some key applications in each area.

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

identify the key types of quantum technologies
discuss basic elements of the operation principles of each type of quantum technology
outline some application areas where quantum technologies are or could be relevant
describe a set of key advantages of quantum technologies over conventional technology.

## Consolidate

Having had an opportunity to discuss the applications of quantum technologies with your peers, your independent study task is:
