This is your opportunity for independent research into the weekly topic. The task might vary from week to week and is likely to take approximately 4 hours to complete.
Browse the Sussex online library portal  (or Web of ScienceLinks to an external site. or ScopusLinks to an external site.) and select two subsectors of quantum technology application to compare and contrast. These will be the same quantum technology subsectors that you chose for your Independent study research activity from Week 1.
Evaluate which nations are most advanced in the production of the science that feeds into those two technologies. You can use the Web of Science ‘analyse’ function, as well as interpreting the results of searches yourself, in order to test some of the hypotheses you may formulate.
Optional extra step: you may wish to download data as a tab delimited file and analyse it using the Bibliometrix R package (or produce VOSviewer bibliometric maps using data downloaded as plain text).
You may want to think about the following questions:
Is quantity more important than quality?
Do citations really capture quality (particularly for more recent publications)?
Is there a bias against non-English speaking nations in the data?
Do we get similar results across nations and firms when we compare the total number of publications, the top 1% cited publications, and publications in top journals such as Nature and Science?
Why do some nations seem to perform better at quantum technology than others – what features of their science funding, delivery and management might drive those differences? For example, why does Switzerland perform so well?
