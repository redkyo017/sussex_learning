The task is:
Browse the Sussex online library portal  (or the Web of ScienceLinks to an external site. or ScopusLinks to an external site.) and search for scientific publications in the two quantum subsectors you have chosen for your portfolio.
Use the ‘analyse’ function to explore the main authors, affiliations, funders and the relative performance of nations. Give attention to changing patterns of scientific outputs, in terms of quality, international linkages and so forth over time, to see the dynamics of the scientific literature.
Step 1: Download the top 500 papers as a tab delimited file with citations included and analyse using the R package Bibliometrix. Find guidance for Bibliometrix documentation is at www.bibliometrix.orgLinks to an external site..
Step 2: Download as a plain text file and use VOSviewer to explore networks of keywords, authors and institutions. Find guidance for VOSviewer at: www.vosviewer.comLinks to an external site. (VOSViewer is probably more user friendly than Bibliometrix unless you have a reasonable knowledge of R).
Step 3: Rework the files to explore and visualise changes over time. If you are able to and have time, have a look to see if there are many firms producing scientific papers across two or more countries of your choice.
Write up a short summary of interesting findings for the Discussion board, which can cover:
where the most interesting research is taking place in different areas of QT
how this is changing over time
any differences between the quantity of outputs and where the research is done (mention the quality of outputs)
whether citations are a good indicator of quality and what biases they might introduce (Hint: language, hype, methods and similar factors).
