## Why the pass happened

Three doubts were raised about the built document:

1. "Market Opportunity Navigator looks duplicated for Part 2."
2. "What are 'Impact and potential' and 'Challenge' for? The blank does not ask for them,
   and they may be in the wrong place."
3. "VPC and BMC notes are thin — each box prints 3–5 questions and the notes do not answer them."

## Findings

1. **Not a defect.** The blank MON ships one Part 2 scorecard; `fill_mon.py` copies it three
   times so each opportunity is scored. Three copies with different ticks is the tool used
   correctly (Part 2 = "evaluate and compare").
2. **Half right.** *Impact*, *Potential*, *Challenge* ARE the blank's three axes (each with three
   sub-criteria and an Overall score). What the blank does not have is any notes field in Part 2.
   The previous session added two free-text rationales per opportunity ("Impact and potential:",
   "Challenge:") parked at the foot of each card, ~60 lines from the ticks they justify, plus an
   **unlabelled orphan sentence** under Opportunity 1's category ticks ("highest potential, lowest
   challenge of the three…") — the "GOLD MINE —" prefix had been dropped by the build.
3. **Right.** About a third of the printed prompts had no answer (e.g. VPC Gain Creators "expected
   or exceed expectations?", VPC Jobs "do goals change in context?", BMC Revenue "how much will
   each stream contribute?", BMC Key Activities/Resources "what do channels/relationships require?").
