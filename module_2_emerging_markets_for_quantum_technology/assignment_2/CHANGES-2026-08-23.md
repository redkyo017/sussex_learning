## Should Part 2/Part 3 carry three opportunities or two? (settled 2026-08-23)

count itself has never changed (three since `90cf459`). Note that the blank constrains **only
Part 1**, which prints three named slots; Part 2 ships one unnamed scorecard and Part 3 one

1. Part 1 names three and all three are filled; Part 2's instruction is "evaluate and compare

2. Part 3 needs a back-up **and** a growth candidate: Opp 2 is Back-up YES / Growth NO, Opp 3 is
   Back-up NO / Growth YES. Drop either and one role of the Agile Focus dartboard is empty.

   outcome of the assessment.
Cutting Opportunity 3 would save ~120 words (from the current 1723, roughly +15% → +7%).
Rejected: not worth a learning outcome.

## Why Part 2 has Notes at all (settled 2026-08-23)
The blank MON has no Notes field anywhere; every `Notes:` paragraph in the MON is ours (the VPC
and BMC blanks do print one per box, which is why the same word is used). They stay, and stay
beside the Overall scores, because:
1. The brief sizes Item 1 at "equivalent to 500 words". Ticks alone come to ~40 words.
2. The note is the only place in Part 2 that can carry a justification, a Harvard citation or a
   concession — i.e. the only place Critical thinking (20) and Reading and research (20) can be
   awarded. A checkbox is an assertion with no evidence.
3. Placement: Impact / Potential / Challenge are the tool's three units of judgement and Overall X
   is a judgement, not an average, so the note belongs at that tick. One note per sub-criterion
   (39 per document) is more granularity than the tool asks for; notes at the foot of the card is
   what `a6d98f4` moved away from; notes on the primary card only would destroy the comparison.
The asymmetry is deliberate: only Opportunity 1 gets the fourth note under Opportunity Category,
because it carries "judged against each other, not in the abstract".
