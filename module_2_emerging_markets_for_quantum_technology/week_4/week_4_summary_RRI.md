## Summary

The video introduces the concept of **Responsible Research and Innovation (RRI)**, an approach aimed at aligning scientific and technological progress with the needs, values, and expectations of society. It outlines how modern challenges (like climate change, aging populations, and mass immigration) require a more open, transparent, and inclusive scientific system. The video concludes by presenting the **RRI Toolkit**, a collection of resources designed to help various stakeholders implement these principles in real life.

## Key Highlights

* **Societal Alignment [[00:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzHsd1ocnEs&t=24)]:** While scientific progress improves lives, it also raises social and ethical controversies. RRI emphasizes that research and innovation must be properly aligned with the values and expectations of citizens.
* **Openness and Inclusivity [[00:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzHsd1ocnEs&t=52)]:** Science needs to become more reflective, reactive to social needs, and inclusive of diverse viewpoints, expertise, and opinions to help build common, desirable futures.
* **Collaborative Governance [[01:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzHsd1ocnEs&t=71)]:** RRI promotes governing science and technology *with* and *for* society, actively engaging all major actors—including civil society, researchers, educators, policymakers, businesses, and industries.
* **Empowering Future Generations [[01:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzHsd1ocnEs&t=91)]:** The approach facilitates equal access to research results and fosters education systems that empower citizens and researchers to make informed decisions for a sustainable society.
* **The RRI Toolkit [[01:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzHsd1ocnEs&t=119)]:** To bridge the gap between science and society, the RRI Toolkit provides practical resources and connects a community of people who share a vision for responsible innovation.
