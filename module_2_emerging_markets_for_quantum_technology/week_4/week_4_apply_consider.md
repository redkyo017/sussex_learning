You may wish to consider the following in your analysis:

the relative benefits and risks to society of innovation
the extent of uncertainty of successful innovation
the potential economic, political and social consequences of innovation and the extent to which they might be being overhyped or underplayed by powerful actors
how the RRI framework and implementation can be improved
the extent to which RRI is an effective approach to technology governance (does is address innovation in other nations; are the costs evenly distributed; does it change incentives effectively).
