 This week, we will be broadening out from viewing innovation purely in corporate terms to look at its context within society. We will examine concepts such as responsible research and innovation (RRI). We will look at the rate of innovation, how its direction can be influenced. Ultimately, we will understand how the outcomes of innovation, whether positive or negative, are highly uncertain and subject to serendipity. This implies an adaptive regulatory approach that allows for experimentation while guarding against negative outcomes.

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

describe responsible innovation
critically analyse innovations and their potential positive and negative consequences
evaluate whether a research or innovation project is responsible and aligned with RRI principles.
