# Quantum 2.0: Sensing, Metrology, and Secure Communication Strategies (Week 1 - 4) - Extra (subsectors for portfolio)

To master the commercial and technological details of quantum subsectors, this research focuses on \*\*Quantum Sensors and Metrology\*\* and \*\*Quantum Communication and Cryptography\*\*. These "Quantum 2.0" fields are distinguished by their active manipulation of quantum states like entanglement and superposition \[1, 2\].

### 1. Quantum Sensors and Metrology
This subsector leverages the extreme sensitivity of quantum states to detect and measure incredibly weak external signals \[1, 3\].

\*   \*\*Technology Detail:\*\* A primary method in this subsector is \*\*atom interferometry\*\*, which suppresses noise sources to create highly accurate sensors for everyday environments \[4\]. Researchers are actively working on miniaturizing these systems—moving \*\*cold atom systems\*\* out of the laboratory into portable, robust devices \[5\]. 
\*   \*\*Applications:\*\*
    \*   \*\*Civil Engineering:\*\* Mapping underground infrastructure (pipes, cables, and voids) without excavation \[6, 7\].
    \*   \*\*Resource Exploration:\*\* Detecting oil, gas, and mineral deposits in challenging conditions \[8\].
    \*   \*\*Disaster Prediction:\*\* Using gravimeters in volcanology to monitor magma intrusion and predict eruptions, or for better flood prediction \[9, 10\].
    \*   \*\*Navigation:\*\* Providing unjammable, \*\*GPS-free navigation\*\* for autonomous vehicles, fighter pilots, and underwater submersibles \[11, 12\].
\*   \*\*Markets:\*\* The \*\*construction industry\*\* is a major target, as roadworks caused by unidentified buried pipes cost businesses in London alone nearly \*\*£1 billion annually\*\* \[13\]. Other key markets include the \*\*$2.5 trillion oil and gas industry\*\*, defense, and aerospace \[14\].
\*   \*\*Main Firms Involved:\*\*
    \*   \*\*e2v (Teledyne e2v):\*\* A key industrial partner focused on translating lab technology into deployable practical devices \[15, 16\].
    \*   \*\*Bridgeporth Geophysics:\*\* A specialist geosciences firm currently field-testing quantum gravimeters against standard commercial tools \[17\].
    \*   \*\*M Squared Lasers:\*\* Collaborating on quantum sensors for gas leak imaging and detection in industrial settings \[18\].

### 2. Quantum Communication and Cryptography
This subsector aims to provide a new standard of security for information processing and transmission, potentially offering encryption that is impossible to crack \[1, 3\].

\*   \*\*Technology Detail:\*\* The cornerstone of this field is \*\*Quantum Key Distribution (QKD)\*\*, which uses quantum effects to allow users to test for the presence of an eavesdropper on a communication channel \[19\]. The system relies on specialized components such as \*\*single-photon sources and detectors\*\* and silicon waveguides \[20, 21\].
\*   \*\*Applications:\*\*
    \*   \*\*Secure Data Links:\*\* Protecting high-bandwidth communication links carrying sensitive government, corporate, or financial data \[22\].
    \*   \*\*E-commerce Security:\*\* Developing \*\*unbreakable digital signatures\*\* to prevent internet fraud, which costs the UK retail sector over £200 million annually \[23\].
    \*   \*\*Financial Infrastructure:\*\* Deploying quantum-protected ATMs and secure networks for high-frequency trading \[24, 25\].
\*   \*\*Markets:\*\* Primary customers are found in \*\*Finance, Telecommunications, Government, and the Military\*\* \[3, 19\]. As the technology becomes more affordable, it is expected to expand to residential customers and general office use \[22\].
\*   \*\*Main Firms Involved:\*\*
    \*   \*\*Toshiba (Toshiba Research Europe Ltd):\*\* A front-runner that has successfully demonstrated QKD systems capable of counting one billion photons per second \[26, 27\].
    \*   \*\*BT (British Telecom):\*\* Partnering on field trials to integrate quantum communications into existing fiber-optic telecom networks \[26\].
    \*   \*\*Nokia and ADVA Optical Network:\*\* International organizations engaged with UK quantum hubs to build connections and explore global export opportunities \[26, 28\].

### Strategic Synthesis: Innovation and Value Capture
Both subsectors exhibit the five attributes of "emerging technologies": \*\*radical novelty\*\*, fast growth, coherence, prominent impact, and high \*\*uncertainty\*\* \[29, 30\]. While the UK and US have strong national strategies to drive these fields, success depends on \*\*complementary assets\*\*—such as established global marketing networks and \*\*intellectual property rights\*\* (e.g., IBM holds over 1,000 quantum-related patents) to ensure firms can capture the value they create \[31-33\].
