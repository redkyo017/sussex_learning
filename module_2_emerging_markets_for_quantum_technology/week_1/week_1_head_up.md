This week, you will be introduced to the structure and learning outcomes of this module and how you will develop essential skills. You will get an overview of the key concepts of innovation and emerging markets. The main types of quantum technology and how innovation can be understood as a process will be covered, as well as an explanation of invention, innovation and diffusion. Key contextual factors that influence success in innovation, such as inter-organisational links and complementary assets, will also be discussed, as well as frameworks for understanding markets and market dynamics.

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

describe the key features of innovation as they apply to quantum markets
critically analyse claims about disruptive innovation
differentiate between invention, innovation and diffusion and the various types of innovation.
