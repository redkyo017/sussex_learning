# Verified external sources — Assignment 1

Verification date: 31 July 2026. Each entry: Claim / Verdict / Publisher / Year / Title / URL / Access date.

## 1. DSIT five quantum missions

- Claim: DSIT announced five quantum missions in November 2023 under the National Quantum Strategy.
- Verdict: VERIFIED (announced 22 November 2023 alongside the Autumn Statement; missions cover quantum computing — 1 trillion operations by 2035, quantum networks, NHS quantum sensing, quantum navigation/PNT, and quantum clocks in critical infrastructure).
- Publisher: Department for Science, Innovation and Technology
- Year: 2023
- Title: National Quantum Strategy Missions
- URL: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-quantum-strategy (missions annex); corroborated by https://www.techuk.org/resource/announcement-of-quantum-missions-in-the-autumn-statement-pushes-the-uk-towards-real-world-deployment-of-quantum-technologies.html
- Access date: 31 July 2026

## 2. NIST post-quantum cryptography standards

- Claim: NIST published its first post-quantum cryptography standards in 2024 (FIPS 203/204/205).
- Verdict: VERIFIED (FIPS 203 ML-KEM, FIPS 204 ML-DSA, FIPS 205 SLH-DSA; effective 14 August 2024; end of a process begun 2016).
- Publisher: National Institute of Standards and Technology
- Year: 2024
- Title: Post-Quantum Cryptography FIPS Approved (FIPS 203, 204, 205)
- URL: https://csrc.nist.gov/news/2024/postquantum-cryptography-fips-approved ; Federal Register notice: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/08/14/2024-17956/
- Access date: 31 July 2026
