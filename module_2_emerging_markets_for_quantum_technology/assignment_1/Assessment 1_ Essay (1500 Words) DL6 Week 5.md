Due on Monday of Week 5 at 10:00 UK time, worth 60 per cent of your overall module grade. This
assessment is 1500 words in length.

**Learning outcomes:**

```

```
## Purpose:

The purpose of this assessment is to check your understanding of what has been covered in Weeks
1–4.

## Details:

Write a 1500-word essay in answer to the prompt: 'How can policymakers and entrepreneurs develop
the UK quantum industry? Propose at least three concrete actions **.'**

The essay should be well-organised and follow a logical structure. It must have a clear introduction
that sets out the purpose and scope of the essay, a well-developed body that covers the key aspects
of the topic chosen, and an interesting conclusion that summarises the main points and draws
insightful conclusions. Please remember to cite sources and include them in your references.

For more guidance on writing essays, visit **University of Sussex Skills Hub, Critical essay writing
(https://www.sussex.ac.uk/skills-hub/writing-and-assessments/#criticalessaywriting)**.
