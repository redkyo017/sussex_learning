## Thesis

The UK is excellent at *creating* quantum value (first national programme 2013; ~3rd globally on quality of quantum science; 2nd to the US in number of quantum companies) but risks failing to *capture* it — the EMI CAT-scanner failure at national scale. Three mutually reinforcing actions close the gap.

## Structure and word budget (~1500 words)

1. **Introduction (~180 w).** The UK paradox; thesis statement; scope (innovation theory + UK National Quantum Strategy 2023).
2. **Diagnosis (~200 w).** Quantum is science-push but the linear model misleads (Kline & Rosenberg 1986; Pavitt 1991). Weak appropriability means complementary assets decide who profits (Teece 1986). EMI CAT scanner as the cautionary case.
3. **Action 1 — Build and share complementary assets (~330 w).** Policymakers: shared fabrication/packaging/testbed infrastructure (NQCC, hubs, missions programme). Entrepreneurs: Teece's decision rule — integrate or partner strategically under weak appropriability; IBM services pivot as the value-capture model. Critical edge: infrastructure must be demand-tied, not speculative supply-push.
4. **Action 2 — Grow and keep the people (~330 w).** Pavitt (1991): trained people are the main economic return on public science because skills are tacit and person-embodied. The 2023 Strategy's 1,000 extra postgraduate researchers; global talent shortage; Korea/Singapore talent-first pressure. Entrepreneurs: hire tacit knowledge, partner with CDTs. Critical edge: training without retention (visas, salaries) leaks value abroad.
5. **Action 3 — Create demand and earn the licence to operate (~330 w).** SAPPHO: user needs = #1 success factor; only 25–33% of UK businesses quantum-ready. Policymakers: government as intelligent lead customer (procurement, PQC migration as demand driver); Rogers' trialability/observability → demonstrators. Entrepreneurs: sell solutions, not qubits. Responsible innovation woven in (Stilgoe/Owen/Macnaghten AREA; Collingridge dilemma; 2018 EPSRC public dialogue — support grows with engagement).
6. **Conclusion (~130 w).** Assets to capture value, people to embody it, demand to realise it; without them the UK repeats EMI at national scale.

## Style rules

- Takes positions and concedes limits; figures used sparingly.
